This assignment made me crash out significantly, and I will probably need several days to recover from these horrors. The idea is simple: three sets of LEDs flashing in three distinct patterns mirroring those of some actual fireflies. In practice though, it did feel a bit rocket-science-esque.

{: .box-note}
**Tip:** I low-key found that writing things out by hand helped a ton. I ended up having a few pages of just messy brainstorming apart from the worksheets we worked on. Then I’d experiment and test as I went, seeing what different code changes did, and adjusting. I think messing up on paper and in the initial stages of coding without aiming for perfection the first time around made me understand the patterns better slowly, and it makes sense why we would want to do this before trying to merge everything into one file.
