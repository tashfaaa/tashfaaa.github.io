I realized after thinking more about my original proposal that my idea to have one switch turn on multiple LEDs at different times might not be very doable. So instead, I've altered the concept slightly: the switch will turn on the three LEDs and make them all blink after specific times to mark reading progress. 

I'm hoping to add some satin stitch (vaguely-book-stack-like) emboroidery on top of the LEDs, with the pink light shining through!
